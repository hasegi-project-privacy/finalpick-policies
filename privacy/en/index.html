Privacy Policy

Final Pick: Ideal Type Bracket (com.hasegi.finalpick)

  • Effective date: November 17, 2026
  • Last updated: November 17, 2026

Hasegi Inc. ("we", "the Company") complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, and publishes this Privacy Policy to explain how information is handled in the app.

Final Pick has no sign-up, no login and no server accounts. We do not receive or store information that identifies you through the app, and there is no field anywhere in the app for your name or email. Everyone shown in the app is a fictional adult created with generative AI, and there is no feature for uploading your own photos.

1. Information We Handle

1.1 Information stored only on your device (never sent to us)

So that you can pick up where you left off, the following is stored in your device's app storage (SharedPreferences and the app's private folder). It is not sent to our servers and we cannot see it.

CategoryStored items
ProgressA stopped bracket (to resume), the candidate group you chose, the date and result of Today's 16
RecordsPeople pinned in your Collection, how often you picked each person and their recent wins, Round of 64 challenges already played
SettingsSound effects, music and vibration, music volume, motion level, language, daily reminder on/off and time, whether you saw the first-launch notice
ProWhether Pro (finalpick_pro) was purchased or restored on this device
Ad pacingNumber of completed brackets, when full-screen and app open ads were last shown and how many times, used to keep the ad intervals
Rating promptWhen we asked for a store rating and your answer
  • We do not collect information that identifies you through the app, such as your name, email, phone number, date of birth, address, location, contacts, photos or voice.
  • The taste report and taste match are calculated on your device; your picks are not sent anywhere.
  • A friend challenge code is a short string containing only bracket information (mode, group and picks). It leaves the app only when you tap Share, and only to the app you choose.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of the above from your device. No backup or recovery is provided.

1.2 Information collected automatically by the ad service

Final Pick shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial, app open, rewarded and native). In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and similar regions, ad consent is collected with Google's consent tool (UMP). In the course of serving ads, Google LLC collects and processes the following directly:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID (AAID) / iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA))
  • Device information (model, OS version, language and region settings, screen information)
  • Network information (approximate location at country or region level estimated from the IP address)
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, and your consent status

We do not collect or store this information ourselves; it is handled under the ad provider's privacy policy.

1.3 In-app purchase information

Payment for Pro (finalpick_pro, a one-time purchase) is processed only through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store (StoreKit).

  • Payment details such as card or bank information are handled by each store; we have no access to them.
  • The app turns Pro on using the purchase confirmation the store sends to your device, and stores only the result on the device. It is not sent to our servers.
  • In the store consoles we can see only aggregate statistics such as sales counts and amounts by country.

1.4 Static file downloads (Cloudflare)

About once a day, the app downloads public static files, such as the list of hidden people, from hasegi-finalpick.pages.dev (Cloudflare Pages). Opening a friend challenge link shows a guide page at the same address.

  • As with any internet request, these requests carry your IP address and basic device or browser information, which Cloudflare, Inc. processes for delivery and security.
  • We do not collect identifying information from these requests and do not keep access logs.
  • If there is no connection or the request fails, the app uses the list bundled inside it.
  • On Android, if you installed the app from a challenge link, the app reads only the challenge code from the Google Play Install Referrer to fill in the code field. This value does not leave your device.

1.5 Emails you send (reports and inquiries)

Report image (in Settings or Collection) and Report an ad (in Settings) open your email app. You can also write to the address below directly. Only if you actually send an email do we receive:

  • Your email address, what you wrote (such as the person number and reason), and the app version and device information if you include them

1.6 Information we do not collect

  • Location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photo or media storage, call or message logs
  • Account information, nicknames, user-generated content such as uploaded photos, chat content
  • The app contains no separate analytics or crash collection tools.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no notification token is sent to a server. Notification permission is requested only for the daily reminder.

3. Retention

  • Information stored on your device: kept on your device until you delete the app or clear its data in device settings. We keep no copy.
  • Advertising information: according to Google LLC's retention policy.
  • Static file requests: according to Cloudflare, Inc.'s retention policy. We keep none.
  • Report and inquiry emails: deleted within one year after we have answered and handled them. Our hiding log keeps only the person number, date and reason, not your email address. Where the law requires longer retention, we follow that period.

5. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

5.1 App data

You control the app data on your device. You can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data in device settings; deleted data cannot be recovered. Pro stays with your store account and can be brought back with Restore purchases in Settings.

5.2 Opting out of personalized ads and resetting the advertising identifier

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • In the app: Settings > Ads & reports > Ad privacy choices (in regions where consent is collected, you can change it here)

On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using the advertising identifier, and turns on ads only after you answer. If you decline, every feature works the same and you see non-personalized ads.

5.3 Ads and your choices

No ads are shown during a bracket or the winner reveal. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one to open the Round of 64. Where and how often ads appear is described in Article 8 of the Terms of Service. With Pro, no ads are shown.

5.4 Age

  • Final Pick is an app for adults aged 18 and over. On Google Play the target audience is declared as 18 and over, and the app does not take part in the Families program.
  • Ad requests do not use the child-directed tag, and ad content is limited to rating G.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 without consent from a legal guardian. If we learn we have received such information (for example, by email), we delete it without delay.

5.5 Contact

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing at any time by emailing the address below. We reply within 10 days of receipt.

6. Security Measures

  • Data minimization: we do not collect information that is not essential to the service. Progress and records stay on your device and are not sent to a server.
  • Protection in transit: ads and static file downloads use encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Access control: we run no user database. Report and inquiry emails are read only by the person in charge.
  • Keeping up to date: we apply security updates to the SDKs we use.
